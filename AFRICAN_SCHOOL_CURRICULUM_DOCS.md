# African School Curriculum (ASC) - Comprehensive Application Document

## 1. Project Overview
**Application Name:** African School Curriculum
**Official Website URL:** [www.africanschoolcurriculum.com](https://www.africanschoolcurriculum.com)
**Mission:** To provide a joyful, interactive, and academically rigorous educational experience for African children, combining modern learning technology with cultural relevance and Christian values.

---

## 2. Target Audience & Educational Structure
The application is designed for students across **Grades 1 through 10** and their parents.

### Grade Categories:
*   **Primary (Grades 1 – 5):** Foundational mastery in Literacy, Numeracy, and Discovery.
*   **Middle School (Grades 6 – 8):** Exploring complex concepts in Science, Social Studies, and Literature.
*   **Senior Levels (Grades 9 – 10):** Advanced preparation and critical thinking for future success.

---

## 3. Comprehensive Curriculum
The curriculum is divided into 3 terms per grade, covering a wide range of subjects:

### Core Subjects:
1.  **Math:** From basic numbers to advanced measurement and data analysis.
2.  **English:** Focusing on Phonics, Grammar, Punctuation, and Comprehension.
3.  **Science:** Topics include God's Creation, The Human Body, Animals, Space, and Technology.
4.  **Social Studies:** Ancient African Empires, Nigerian Independence, Cultural Traditions, and Geography.
5.  **Literature:** Godly Short Stories, Christian Poetry, Parables, and Moral Fables.
6.  **Bible Study:** Creation, Noah, Jezus' Parables, Apostles, and Prophets.
7.  **Spelling:** Phonetic sets, Technical terms, and Greek/Latin roots.
8.  **Alphabet (Grade 1 Only):** Specialized literacy foundation.

---

## 4. Key Application Features

### A. Rapid-Fire Drills
A hallmark feature of ASC is the dynamic testing environment:
*   **20-Question Sessions:** Every drill is standardized to 20 questions to ensure comprehensive review.
*   **Massive Question Pool:** Hundreds of unique questions across all subjects prevent repetition.
*   **Randomization:** Questions and options are shuffled every time a student starts a session.
*   **Subject Cycling:** Upon completion, students are prompted to "Change Drill" to the next subject in the cycle, encouraging well-rounded mastery.

### B. Vast Digital Library
A rich repository of knowledge and imagination:
*   **500+ Books & Novels:** Categorized by genre (Science Fiction, Adventure, Classic, Educational).
*   **Multi-Page Content:** Extensive novels like "The Magic Forest" and "Space Explorers" with detailed chapters.
*   **Christian Stories:** A dedicated focus on spiritual growth and moral narratives.

### C. Student Dashboard & Gamification
*   **Awards System:** Students earn badges based on their average scores (Gold Star, Silver Star, Rising Star).
*   **The SUPER BADGE:** A special achievement unlocked by Grade 10 students who complete 100% of the curriculum.
*   **Graduation Day Program:** A formal ceremony simulation for Grade 10 graduates, including a read-aloud ceremony list and valedictory speech highlights.

### D. Parent Portal (Insight & Tracking)
A dedicated interface for parents to monitor their child's progress:
*   **Real-Time Performance:** Tracking overall progress percentages and average scores.
*   **Subject Breakdown:** Seeing which subjects the child excels in or struggles with.
*   **Learning Alerts:** Parents are notified of "Critical Failures" (scores ≤ 60%) where a student was forced to restart a session.
*   **Platform Events:** Updates on curriculum changes and new feature launches.

## 5. Visual Brand Identity
The application features a distinct "Atmospheric" design:
*   **Professional Typography:** A blend of *Inter*, *Playfair Display*, and *Space Grotesk*.
*   **Joyful UI:** Bright primary colors, "pillowy" shadows, and interactive animations using `motion`.
*   **Signature Background:** A persistent decorative layout featuring soft color blobs and a professional dot-grid pattern.
